About this preview

Depth is positive below low tide. Negative change means shallowing; positive change means deepening.

Predicted view shows eligible cells with measured starting depths. Observed and error views show only cells measured in both surveys. Blank areas mean missing data, not safe water.

Error is predicted depth minus observed depth. Positive (red) means the model predicted water too deep; negative (blue) means it predicted water too shallow. The selected-date errors compare identical measured cells against retaining the starting depth.

MAE measures average absolute error on held-out survey dates; it is not a confidence interval. Forecasts alone do not determine when dredging is required.

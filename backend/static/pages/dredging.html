Reading this preview

Depth is positive below low tide. A negative change means shallowing; a positive change means deepening.

Only eligible cells with a measured starting depth are shown. Empty areas are outside this prediction footprint, not zero depth.

These are historical forecasts, not a live survey or navigation chart. Predicting a dredging date needs required channel depths and intervention records.

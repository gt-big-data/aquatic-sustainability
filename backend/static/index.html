About the Project

This project is focused on identifying patterns in water-related disasters and affordability across different communities using advanced data analytics. By analyzing watershed data, economic indicators, and environmental factors, we developed predictive models to identify at-risk communities and provide actionable insights for sustainable water management and policy decisions.
